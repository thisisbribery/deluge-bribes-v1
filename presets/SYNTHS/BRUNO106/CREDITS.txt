BRUNO106 presets converted from KR-106's preset data

These presets are converted from the preset data that ships with Ultramaster KR-106
by Karl LaRocca (https://github.com/kayrockscreenprinting/ultramaster_kr106),
licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0. That data includes the
Juno-106 factory patches and David Churcher's patch library (made for the
original Ultramaster KR-106). The converted presets are distributed under the
same licence, GPL-3.0.

Converted with synth-tools/gen_bruno106_kr.py: KR-106's own parameter curves
(its hardware-calibrated Juno-106 curves for the factory patches, the original
Ultramaster scaling for Churcher's), mapped onto the Deluge's BRUNO106 engine
and its envelopes, LFO and filter. LFO delay is not carried over (the Deluge
has no equivalent). Bank codes are dropped from the names.

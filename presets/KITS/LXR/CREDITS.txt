LXR preset kits for Deluge Bribes (KITS/LXR, numbers 16-41)
============================================================

16-25  EntropiaLabs kits, converted
-----------------------------------
16 TEKKNO, 17 BOOM808, 18 ELECTRO, 19 LOFIDUST, 20 INDUSTRL, 21 MINIMAL (bank ELABS)
22 NTRPBT1, 23 CONCRETO, 24 FERRUGEM, 25 MADRUGA (project ENTROPIA)

By EntropiaLabs (GitHub: mcamaradev), from ELABS RLX, a VST3 of the LXR-02 drum engine:
  https://github.com/mcamaradev/ELABS-RLX-02-LXR02-CLONE-VST  (kits/elabs/ELABS, kits/elabs/ENTROPIA)
  commit 8d7d7023068e1dc46a79e0fcf945089bb626092d (fetched 2026-11-15)
The repository is licensed GPL-3.0 (LICENSE at its root) and its README says the kits are its own work ("11 kits
and 7 patterns by EntropiaLabs built in"); unlike the LXR-02 factory content, which it says it does not ship, no
other licence or exception is stated for them. They are used here under GPL-3.0.
Left out: CHAPA (its voices are the LXR-02 init kit with one tom changed; the rest of it is FX) and 00-NTRPB.SND
(a byte-identical copy of NTRPBT1).

26-41  Newly designed kits
--------------------------
26 DEEP808, 27 PUNCH909, 28 TIGHT606, 29 CR78SOFT, 30 FOUNDRY, 31 CLICKS, 32 BRONX, 33 DUBPLATE, 34 TAPEDUST,
35 SUBBOOM, 36 TWOSTEP, 37 HEXPADS, 38 BELLTREE, 39 AMENISH, 40 ACIDHAUS, 41 BITROT

Not converted from anyone's kit: written for Deluge Bribes in tools/design_kits.py, each starting from the LXR-02
firmware's init kit (erica-synths/lxr-desktop-app, Core/Src/Preset/initKit.c, Copyright (C) 2012-2026 Erica
Synths, GPL-3.0-only) with new per-voice settings. They are also valid LXR-02 kits (designed-snd/*.SND).
Not yet listened to on hardware.

Row 8, PERC (all kits, from Deluge Bribes v1.40)
------------------------------------------------
Every kit's eighth row is its own HTOM row a fifth up with a shorter decay, added by tools/add_perc_rows.py
(thisisbribery/deluge-bribes-v1 tools/, GPL-3.0). It is not in the LXR-02 kit the rest was converted from.

Tools
-----
tools/lxr_to_deluge.py and tools/lxr02_params.json: the Bribes converter (thisisbribery/deluge-bribes-v1 tools/,
GPL-3.0), unchanged; it reproduces the published Hrtl kits byte for byte. The LXR-02 parameter order it uses was
checked against erica-synths/lxr-desktop-app Core/Inc/Parameters.h (all 247 sound parameters match).
The rest of tools/ was written for this folder (GPL-3.0, as the firmware).

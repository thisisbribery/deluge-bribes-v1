# Cycles presets

Sixteen kits by [thisisbribery](https://github.com/thisisbribery): bank E of my own Model:Cycles kits, made on my own machine and converted to Deluge kits on the drum synth's Cyc engines. Each row's machine, Color, Shape, Sweep, Contour, decay, Punch, pitch and level go across; the Deluge envelope is open with a release long enough for each sound's own decay (gated sounds keep their short release), and delay and reverb sends are left off.

Copy this folder to `KITS/CYCLES` on the Deluge's SD card. On a Cycles kit, **SHIFT + KIT** browses them.
